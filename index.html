About

I work at the intersection of talent acquisition, people operations and business strategy. My focus is helping organisations identify professionals who can do the work, strengthen the culture and contribute to the bigger picture.

My experience spans technical and nontechnical recruitment, discreet search, distributed team hiring and people operations. I combine structured sourcing, behavioural assessment and project management principles to keep hiring focused and aligned with the needs of the business.

Across fintech, technology, education and oil and gas, I have built hiring around people and business goals, in teams spread across EMEA, APAC and LATAM.
